Settings › General (Text size added under Appearance)
Text size is the body text size in points: a whole number from 9 to 18, default 12. − and + change it by 1. Other text, row heights, avatars and the popover and window widths follow it in proportion. It applies to every account. In the popover and the window, ⌘= (or ⌘+) makes it 1 pt larger, ⌘− 1 pt smaller, and ⌘0 sets it back to 12.
